> [!NOTE]
> **Academic Integrity Audit**:
> - **Integrated Science Respected**: Classes 6–10 map exclusively to integrated NCERT **Science**, **Mathematics**, or **Social Science (Geography)** textbooks—never invented standalone middle-school Physics/Chemistry/Biology books.
> - **Senior Secondary Precision**: Classes 11–12 map separate **Physics** textbooks (`keph1`, `keph2`, `leph2`) where quantitative formulas (hydrostatic pressure $P = \rho g h$, torque $\tau = r \times F$, semiconductor solar cells) formally appear.
> - **Dropped Rationalised Chapters Excluded**: Permanently dropped chapters (such as old Class 10 Science Ch. 14 *Sources of Energy*) are strictly excluded; solar/renewable energy is accurately mapped to Class 6 *Curiosity* Ch. 11 (*Nature's Treasures*), Class 10 Geography Ch. 5 (*Minerals and Energy Resources*), Class 10 Science Ch. 11 (*Electricity*), and Class 12 Physics Ch. 14 (*Semiconductor Electronics*).
> - **Honest Interdisciplinary Extension Flagging**: Quantitative pulley/gear mechanical advantage (`EXT_SIMPLE_MACHINES_PULLEY`) is explicitly flagged `interdisciplinary_extension` anchored to Class 8 Science Ch. 5 (*Exploring Forces*) rather than claiming a standalone simple-machines NCERT chapter.
